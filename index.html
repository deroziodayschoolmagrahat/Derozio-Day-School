Our school

A strong beginning for a brighter future.

Established in 2009, Derozio Day School aims to provide a welcoming learning environment where children can develop knowledge, confidence, creativity, and good values.

  • Learning with curiosity
  • Respect and responsibility
  • Creativity and expression
  • A supportive environment
